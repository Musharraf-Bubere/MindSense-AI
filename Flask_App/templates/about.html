🧠 About MindSense AI

AI-Powered Mental Health Risk Prediction using Explainable Artificial Intelligence (SHAP)

MindSense AI is an intelligent web application designed to predict an individual's mental health risk using Machine Learning while providing transparent explanations through SHAP (Explainable AI). The system helps users understand why predictions are made and provides personalized recommendations for better mental well-being.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make mental health assessment more accessible, transparent, and trustworthy by combining Machine Learning with Explainable Artificial Intelligence. We believe every prediction should be understandable so users can make informed decisions about their mental well-being.

✨ Key Features

🤖

AI Prediction

Uses Logistic Regression to predict mental health risk with high accuracy.

🧠

Explainable AI

Every prediction is explained using SHAP feature importance.

💙

Recommendations

Personalized suggestions based on user responses and predicted risk.

📊

Analytics Dashboard

Interactive charts and real-time assessment statistics.
